## Current Research

My research examines the historical lives of concepts: how words acquire meanings, carry cultural memories, and reflect changing forms of social experience.

My current work focuses on language, conceptual history, and the transformations of selfhood in modernity, with particular attention to the relationship between linguistic change, cultural memory, and emerging technologies.
